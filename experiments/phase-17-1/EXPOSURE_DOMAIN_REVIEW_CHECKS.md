## Findings addressed in the final design

1. Costing from Phase 16 update throughput understated full supervision/evidence
   cost. Use the measured 4,559-second training/preflight scope, retain both timing
   scopes and do not attribute their difference to a particular GPU kernel.
2. A simple one-versus-four-pass comparison changes the learning-rate schedule too.
   Add a fixed, ineligible first-pass diagnostic in the long trajectory and state
   the remaining confound explicitly. Do not claim pure exposure causality or a
   domain-coverage test. Four passes remain a bounded design choice, not an optimum.
3. Distinguish 100% encyclopedia pretraining from the separate instruction corpus;
   distinguish executed document-layout targets from Phase 15 record accounting.
   Reject old-book mixture clearance by assumption and model-size escalation based
   only on available memory. Neither exposure nor domain coverage is an established
   cause. No new mixture, tokenization or acquisition was performed.
4. Bound preparation optimization: zero-update scripted preparation plus at most
   the existing 52 CUDA preflight updates in a future authorized study. Additional
   optimizer fixtures would require a revised proposal, not an implicit budget.
5. Clarify control source/numerical binding, final candidate eligibility, absence of
   serious replay permission, stop on failed fixed acceptance, and owner review
   before implementation. No alternate checkpoint promotion or final access.
6. Correct current state for the verified published closure and the now-authorized
   completed review; retain all permanent negative dispositions and future gates.
