Every visitor received a numbered ticket at the entrance to the exhibition. The volunteers explained where to leave wet coats, pointed out the drinking fountain, and offered a printed map of the rooms.

The observatory opens its western window after sunset so students can compare the changing sky with their drawings. They record the date, the weather, and the direction of each bright object before making another observation the following month.

A pair of musicians met at the station with a violin and a small drum. They tried a slow melody while waiting for their train, then changed the rhythm when a child began tapping her shoes against the bench.

Rainwater collected in the barrel beside the greenhouse throughout the night. In the morning, the gardener used a marked jug to measure the supply before watering the seedlings in their separate clay pots.
